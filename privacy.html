Privacy Policy for Word Solitaire

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Word Solitaire ("the App") is a solitaire-style vocabulary game. This policy explains what data the App does — and does not — collect.

1. No personal data collected

The App does not require creating an account or signing in. It does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information — no name, email address, phone number, location, or device identifier.

2. Local storage only

Your progress (coins earned, unlocked levels, and remaining lives) is saved only on your own device, using standard local device storage. This data never leaves your device, is never sent to us, and is never shared with any third party. Uninstalling the App permanently deletes this data.

3. No analytics or tracking

The App does not use analytics services, advertising networks, or any tracking technology.

4. Third-party services

The App loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. Displaying the App's screens may cause your device to request font files from Google's servers, which can log standard technical information such as your IP address, subject to Google's own privacy policy. The App itself does not send any personal data as part of this request.

5. Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to this policy

Any future changes to this policy will be posted on this page, with the "Last updated" date above changed accordingly.
